Good to know

Charges & Policies

Transport charges that sit outside the basic freight. Values below are the indicative demo defaults maintained in the admin rate card — confirm each one with the office before dispatch.

Detention / standby

Charged per day when a vehicle is held beyond the agreed loading/unloading time. The demo rate card uses ₹1,500 per day; actuals depend on the trip and are confirmed with you.

Loading & unloading

Loading/unloading shown in the rate card covers normal manual handling for the truck category. Crane, hydra, extra labour or special packing is charged at actuals.

Toll, parking & permits

State tolls, parking, entry fees and route permits are charged at actuals with receipts. For repeat lanes these can be built into the freight instead — ask the office.

GST on freight

Freight is quoted exclusive of GST unless stated otherwise. The demo rate card applies 5% (GTA service) — the correct rate for your consignment must be verified by the accounts team.

E-way bill & documents

For goods above the statutory threshold, a valid e-way bill and invoice are required before dispatch. The booking form lets you record invoice and e-way bill numbers for the lorry receipt.

Transit insurance

Cargo is carried at owner's risk unless transit insurance is arranged separately. Declare fragile, hazardous or high-value goods clearly while booking so the right vehicle and cover can be arranged.
